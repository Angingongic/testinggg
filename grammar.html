Definite and Indefinite Articles

Romanian uses enclitic definite articles that attach to the end of nouns. For example, băiat (boy) becomes băiatul (the boy). Indefinite articles precede the noun, e.g. un băiat (a boy).

Present Tense of Regular Verbs

Regular verbs in Romanian end in -a, -e or -i. The present tense endings depend on the conjugation. For example, verbs ending in -a like a lucra (to work) are conjugated: lucrez, lucrezi, lucrează...
