Romanian Travel Phrases

"Bună ziua" is a common greeting used throughout the day.

RomanianPod101 encourages mastering polite phrases for travel.

"Unde este gara?" means "Where is the train station?"

"Cât costă?" asks "How much does it cost?"

"Nu vorbesc românește foarte bine" means "I don't speak Romanian very well."

"Ajutor!" is the word for "Help!"

"Vreau să merg la hotel" means "I want to go to the hotel."

"Îmi puteți face o recomandare?" asks for a recommendation.

"Mi-e foame" expresses hunger.

"O notă de plată, vă rog" is used in restaurants.

"La revedere" is the standard farewell.

"Bilet dus-întors" means "round-trip ticket."

"Pot plăti cu cardul?" asks if you can pay by card.

"Baie" is the word for bathroom.

"Direcția" refers to direction.

RomanianPod101 provides pronunciation tips for these phrases.

"Vorbiți engleză?" means "Do you speak English?"

"Aș dori o hartă" is "I'd like a map."

"Cât de departe este?" asks "How far is it?"

"Poftim?" politely asks someone to repeat themselves.

"Rezervare" means reservation, useful for hotels.

"Trebuie să schimb bani" means "I need to exchange money."

"O cameră single" is a single room.

"Se acceptă animale?" asks if pets are allowed.

"Unde pot lua un taxi?" is helpful for travelers.

RomanianPod101 includes audio of taxi conversations.

"Ajungeți acolo în zece minute" means you'll reach there in ten minutes.

"Bagaj" means luggage.

"Plaja" is "beach," a common word for summer trips.

Learning these phrases in context makes traveling smoother.
