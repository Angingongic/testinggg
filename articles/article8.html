Traditional Romanian cuisine features dishes like sarmale.

"Mămăligă" is a popular cornmeal porridge.

RomanianPod101 offers vocabulary lists for meals and ingredients.

"Ciorbă" refers to a sour soup.

Meats include "pui" for chicken and "porc" for pork.

"Pește" means fish.

Breads are called "pâine."

A sweet pastry is "cozonac."

"Brânză" means cheese, commonly sheep's cheese.

"Fructe" is the word for fruits.

Vegetables are "legume."

"Usturoi" is garlic, used in many recipes.

"Ceapă" stands for onion.

RomanianPod101 highlights the phrase "Poftă bună!" meaning "Enjoy your meal."

Beverages include "vin" for wine and "apă" for water.

"Bere" means beer.

Desserts like "clătite" are popular.

Many meals start with an appetizer or "gustare."

"Desert" is the word for dessert.

Markets are known as "piață."

"Cartofi" means potatoes, often served fried.

"Salată" is salad.

Spices include "piper" and "sare."

RomanianPod101 provides dialogues about shopping for food.

Street food features "mici," grilled meat rolls.

Restaurants offer both "meniul zilei" (daily menu) and à la carte.

Breakfast often includes cold cuts and cheese.

Vegetarians will find dishes like "zacuscă," a vegetable spread.

Many families cook soup daily.

Exploring Romanian recipes helps expand vocabulary and cultural understanding.
