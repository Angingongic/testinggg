Romanian uses personal, possessive, demonstrative, relative, interrogative and reflexive pronouns.

RomanianPod101 notes that personal pronouns are often dropped because verbs show person clearly.

The pronoun "eu" means "I" and is first-person singular.

"Tu" stands for "you" in informal contexts.

"El" and "ea" correspond to "he" and "she."

"Noi" is the pronoun for "we."

"Voi" refers to "you" plural or formal.

"Ei" is "they" for masculine groups, and "ele" for feminine groups.

Possessive pronouns must agree in gender and number with the possessed noun.

For example, "cartea mea" means "my book."

RomanianPod101 suggests learning the four forms of each possessive pronoun.

Demonstrative pronouns such as "acesta" (this) vary by gender and number.

"Acela" refers to "that" at a distance.

Relative pronouns like "care" introduce subordinate clauses.

Interrogative pronouns include "cine?" for "who?" and "ce?" for "what?"

Reflexive pronouns "mă", "te", "se" are used with reflexive verbs.

Some pronouns have clitic forms that attach to the verb.

These clitics can appear before or after the verb depending on syntax.

"Mi" and "ți" show indirect objects for first and second person.

Double object constructions often use both a noun and a pronoun for emphasis.

RomanianPod101 points out that polite address uses "dumneavoastră."

This form takes third-person verb conjugation.

Stressed forms of pronouns can stand alone for emphasis.

Unstressed forms typically precede the verb.

The negative pronoun "nimeni" means "nobody."

"Nimic" translates as "nothing."

Indefinite pronouns like "cineva" mean "someone."

There are also adverbial pronouns such as "unde" (where).

Mastery of pronoun placement requires practice with real sentences.

Listening to dialogues on RomanianPod101 helps internalize correct usage.
