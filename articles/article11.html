Romanian Workplace Vocabulary

"Birou" means office.

RomanianPod101 shares common workplace expressions.

"Ședință" stands for meeting.

"Angajat" is employee, while "angajator" is employer.

"Salariu" means salary.

"Programul" refers to working hours.

"Contract de muncă" is an employment contract.

"Sarcină" means task.

"Termen limită" is deadline.

"Promovare" means promotion.

"Echipă" is team.

"Colega" or "coleg" is coworker.

"Biroul de resurse umane" is the HR office.

"Concediu" means vacation or leave.

"Șomaj" refers to unemployment.

"A lucra de acasă" means to work from home.

"Laptop" remains the same in Romanian.

"Șef" means boss or manager.

"Proiect" is project.

RomanianPod101 demonstrates polite forms for addressing superiors.

"E-mail" or "mail" refers to electronic mail.

"Întâlnire online" is an online meeting.

"Raport" stands for report.

"Plan de afaceri" means business plan.

"Termeni și condiții" are terms and conditions.

"Punctualitate" is punctuality, valued in professional settings.

"Biroul deschis" translates to open office.

"Bonus" is a financial bonus.

"Cont bancar" means bank account.

Practicing workplace phrases with RomanianPod101 aids professional communication.
