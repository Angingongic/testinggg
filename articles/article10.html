"Mamă" means mother.

"Tată" means father.

RomanianPod101 lists extended family vocabulary.

"Frate" is brother and "soră" is sister.

Grandparents are "bunică" and "bunic."

"Unchi" stands for uncle, while "mătușă" is aunt.

A cousin is "verișor" for males or "verișoară" for females.

"Nepot" can mean grandson or nephew.

"Nepoată" is granddaughter or niece.

"Soț" is husband and "soție" is wife.

"Părinți" refers to parents.

RomanianPod101 explains cultural aspects of family gatherings.

"Ginere" means son-in-law.

"Noră" means daughter-in-law.

Extended family may gather on holidays like Easter.

"Cumnat" and "cumnată" mean brother- and sister-in-law.

"Străbunic" is great-grandfather.

"Străbunică" is great-grandmother.

Family names are often passed down through generations.

"Naș" and "nașă" refer to godparents.

RomanianPod101 describes baptism traditions.

"Fin" or "fiu spiritual" is a godchild.

"Căsătorie" is marriage.

"Divorț" is divorce.

Many households include "copii" meaning children.

"Văr primar" indicates a first cousin.

Family ties are important in Romanian society.

Formal gatherings may include the extended "rudenie."

"Înrudit" means related by blood.

Practicing these terms on RomanianPod101 helps with family conversations.
