The present tense is used for habitual and current actions.

Regular verbs in the first conjugation end in -a.

For these verbs, the first-person singular ends in -ez or -esc.

Second conjugation verbs typically end in -ea.

They drop the -ea and add endings like -ez or -ezi.

RomanianPod101 offers charts for each conjugation pattern.

Third conjugation verbs end in -e.

Their present forms use endings like -esc or -ești.

Fourth conjugation verbs end in -i or -î.

These verbs add -esc in first-person singular, though there are exceptions.

Irregular verbs must be memorized individually.

"A fi" uses forms like "sunt" and "este."

"A avea" uses "am," "ai," "are," etc.

The present tense also expresses future events with time adverbs.

Negative forms use "nu" before the verb.

Questions may be formed with rising intonation or with question words.

Pronouns are optional because verb endings indicate subject.

Reflexive verbs combine with a reflexive pronoun.

RomanianPod101 recommends listening to native speakers for pronunciation.

Stem changes may occur in some verbs before the ending.

Many verbs use -iți for the second-person plural.

The first-person plural often ends in -ăm or -em.

When addressing someone formally, you use the second-person plural forms.

Contractions are common in spoken language.

The present tense pairs with adverbs such as "acum" (now).

Habitual actions may pair with "în mod obișnuit."

Some verbs are defective and missing certain forms.

Vocabulary lists on RomanianPod101 provide practice sentences.

Keep a journal in Romanian to practice the present tense.

Daily usage is the best way to master these conjugations.
